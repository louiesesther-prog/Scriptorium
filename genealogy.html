THE WOMEN IN THE LINE — MATTHEW 1

TAMAR

Genesis 38 — Judah's daughter-in-law
📖 Matthew 1:3
A Canaanite woman who preserved the line through Judah when justice failed. Her son Perez continued the Messianic line. A type of the outsider brought into the covenant.

RAHAB

Joshua 2, 6 — The harlot of Jericho
📖 Matthew 1:5
A Canaanite prostitute who hid the Israelite spies and became an ancestor of David. Her faith is celebrated in Hebrews 11:31 and James 2:25. A sign that grace transcends ethnic boundaries.

RUTH

Book of Ruth — The Moabitess
📖 Matthew 1:5
A Moabite widow whose loyalty to Naomi led her to Bethlehem. Her kinsman-redeemer Boaz married her. A Gentile grandmother of David, demonstrating God's love for the nations.

BATHSHEBA

2 Samuel 11-12 — Wife of Uriah
📖 Matthew 1:6 ("her who had been the wife of Uriah")
Taken by David in adultery, yet through her Solomon was born. Matthew deliberately avoids naming her, instead emphasizing Uriah — a perpetual reminder of David's sin and God's forgiveness.

MARY (MIRIAM)

The Theotokos — God-bearer
📖 Matthew 1:16, Luke 1:26-38
The climax of the women in the line. A virgin of Nazareth who conceived by the Holy Spirit. Through her "yes," God became flesh. Her lineage through Nathan (Luke 3) provides the biological descent of Jesus, complementing Joseph's legal line through Solomon.

Matthew's genealogy traces the legal throne-right through Solomon.
Luke's genealogy traces the biological descent through Nathan, son of David.
