I. THE TORAH — FIVE BOOKS OF MOSES

001 — THE TORAH
GENESIS
THE BOOK OF BEGINNINGS
"In the beginning God created..." Creation, the fall, the covenant with Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and the Twelve Patriarchs — the roots of all Scripture.
SITE: Nablus, Palestine (c. 4th cent. AD)
002 — THE TORAH
EXODUS
OUT OF EGYPT
"Let my people go." The redemption from slavery, the giving of the Law at Sinai, the tabernacle, and theAaronic priesthood.
SITE: Egypt (c. 150 BC)
003 — THE TORAH
LEVITICUS
THE HOLY GOD
"Be holy, for I am holy." The sacrificial system, the Day of Atonement, the priesthood, and the call to relational holiness.
SITE: Qumran Cave 4 (1947)
004 — THE TORAH
NUMBERS
WILDERNESS WALK
The census, the journey through the wilderness, the rebellions of Korah and Balaam, and the plains of Moab.
SITE: Qumran Cave 4 (1947)
005 — THE TORAH
DEUTERONOMY
THE SECOND LAW
Moses's three speeches — the law reviewed, blessings and curses declared, and theSong of Moses on the plains of Moab.
SITE: Qumran Cave 4 (1947)

II. HISTORICAL BOOKS

006 — HISTORICAL
JOSHUA
INTO THE PROMISED LAND
"As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD." The conquest of Canaan, the division of the land, and Joshua's farewell.
SITE: Qumran Cave 5 (1952)
007 — HISTORICAL
JUDGES
EVERY MAN DID WHAT WAS RIGHT
"In those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did what was right in his own eyes." Six cycles of apostasy, oppression, and deliverance.
SITE: Qumran Cave 4 (1947)
008 — HISTORICAL
RUTH
KINDNESS & REDEMPTION
"Where you go, I will go." A Moabite woman in the genealogy of David — kindness, loyalty, and the kinsman-redeemer.
SITE: Qumran Cave 4 (1947)
009 — HISTORICAL
1 SAMUEL
THE ANOINTED KING
Samuel's birth, the ark's capture, Saul's kingship, and David's rise — the transition from judges to monarchy.
SITE: Qumran Cave 4 (1947)
010 — HISTORICAL
2 SAMUEL
DAVID'S REIGN
David's kingdom established, Bathsheba's sin, Absalom's rebellion, and the deuterocanonical 'Songs of the Bow.'
SITE: Qumran Cave 4 (1947)
011 — HISTORICAL
1 KINGS
SOLOMON'S GLORY & DIVISION
Solomon's wisdom, the Temple's construction, the divided kingdom, and Elijah's confrontation with the prophets of Baal.
SITE: Qumran Cave 4 (1947)
012 — HISTORICAL
2 KINGS
FALL OF THE KINGDOMS
Elisha's miracles, the fall of Israel to Assyria, Hezekiah's tunnel, Josiah's reform, and the exile to Babylon.
SITE: Qumran Cave 4 (1947)
013 — HISTORICAL
1 CHRONICLES
THE KINGDOM'S MEMORY
Adam to David — genealogies, Saul's failure, and Solomon's Temple preparations. The Chronicler's liturgical history.
SITE: Qumran Cave 4 (1947)
014 — HISTORICAL
2 CHRONICLES
SOLOMON TO THE EXILE
Solomon's Temple, the divided monarchy, Hezekiah and Isaiah, Josiah's discovery of the Book of the Law, and the Babylonian exile.
SITE: Qumran Cave 4 (1947)
015 — HISTORICAL
EZRA
RETURN FROM BABYLON
The decree of Cyrus, the rebuilding of the Temple, Ezra's reform of mixed marriages, and the reading of the Law.
SITE: Qumran Cave 4 (1947)
016 — HISTORICAL
NEHEMIAH
WALLS REBUILT
"Shall there cease to be a remnant?" Nehemiah surveys the ruins, the walls are rebuilt in 52 days, and the covenant is renewed.
SITE: Qumran Cave 4 (1947)
017 — HISTORICAL
ESTHER
FOR SUCH A TIME AS THIS
"Who knows whether you have not come to the kingdom for such a time as this?" A queen saves her people from Haman's genocide.
SITE: No Qumran manuscripts found

III. MAJOR PROPHETS

023 — MAJOR PROPHET
ISAIAH
THE HOLY ONE OF ISRAEL
"For unto us a child is born." The most quoted Old Testament prophet — judgment against Judah, the Servant songs, Immanuel's birth, and the new heavens and new earth.
SITE: Qumran Cave 1 (1947)
024 — MAJOR PROPHET
JEREMIAH
THE WEEPING PROPHET
"I know the plans I have for you." Jeremiah's warnings to Judah, the new covenant promise, the valley of dry bones, and Jerusalem's fall to Babylon.
025 — MAJOR PROPHET
LAMENTATIONS
THE CITY IN ASHES
"How lonely sits the city." Five acrostic poems of grief over Jerusalem's destruction — anguish and hope interweaved in the prophet's cry to a faithful God.
026 — MAJOR PROPHET
EZEKIEL
VISION OF THE GLORY
The glory departs from the Temple, the valley of dry bones brought to life, the shepherd and the sheep, and the new Temple with water flowing from its threshold.
027 — MAJOR PROPHET
DANIEL
THE LION'S DEN
The furnace and the stone cut without hands, the seventy weeks, the sea creature vision, and the ancient of days seated on the throne of glory.

IV. MINOR PROPHETS

028 — MINOR PROPHET
HOSEA
GOD'S UNFAILING LOVE
"Go, take to yourself a wife of whoredom." Hosea's marriage as a living parable of God's faithfulness to unfaithful Israel — love that refuses to let go.
029 — MINOR PROPHET
JOEL
THE DAY OF THE LORD
"The day of the LORD is great and very terrifying." The locust plague, the outpouring of the Spirit, and the final victory when the Lord dwells in Zion.
030 — MINOR PROPHET
AMOS
JUSTICE ROLLS DOWN
"Let justice roll down like waters." A shepherd's message from Tekoa — judgment on the rich, the complacent, and the hollow worship that conceals oppression.
031 — MINOR PROPHET
OBADIAH
EDOM'S PRIDE FALLS
The shortest prophecy — Edom's betrayal of Jacob, the downfall of the mountain of Esau, and the saving of the house of Jacob.
032 — MINOR PROPHET
JONAH
THE RELUCTANT MESSENGER
"Go to Nineveh." The storm, the great fish, Jonah's anger at God's mercy, and the city that turned from its evil — the most universal of all prophetic books.
033 — MINOR PROPHET
MICAH
THE RULER FROM BETHLEHEM
"But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah... from you shall come forth one who is to be ruler." Justice, kindness, and humility called for — and the promise of the Messianic king.
034 — MINOR PROPHET
NAHUM
THE FALL OF NINEVEH
"The LORD is slow to anger and great in power." The overthrow of Nineveh, the burden of Nineveh, and the contrast with the just and patient God.
035 — MINOR PROPHET
HABAKKUK
THE JUST SHALL LIVE BY FAITH
"Why do you idly look at ruin?" The prophet's complaint, God's answer through the Chaldeans, and Habakkuk's final prayer of faith: "The Lord is in his holy temple."
036 — MINOR PROPHET
ZEPHANIAH
THE DAY OF WRATH
"Seek the LORD, all you humble of the land." The day of the LORD — judgment, but also a remnant, a humble and lowly people, who find rest in the Lord's love.
037 — MINOR PROPHET
HAGGAI
REBUILD THE TEMPLE
"Is it a time for you yourselves to dwell in your paneled houses while this house lies in ruins?" Haggai calls the people to prioritize the rebuilding of the Temple.
038 — MINOR PROPHET
ZECHARIAH
THE VISIONARY PROPHET
Eight visions of the night, the horsemen among the myrtle trees, the flying scroll, and the King riding on a colt — apocalyptic imagery pointing to the coming Messiah.
039 — MINOR PROPHET
MALACHI
THE SUN OF RIGHTEOUSNESS
"Have we not all one Father?" Crippled sacrifices, divorce, robbery, and the final promise — the sun of righteousness rising with healing in its wings — before the silence of 400 years.

V. WISDOM LITERATURE

018 — WISDOM
JOB
THE TEMPEST OF AFFLICTION
"The LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away." The integrity of a man tested beyond measure — three friends, one whisper from the whirlwind, and the sovereignty of God.
019 — WISDOM
PSALMS
THE PRAYER BOOK OF ISRAEL
150 songs of Israel — lament, praise, royal coronation, pilgrimage, wisdom, and messianic prophecy. The most read and most quoted book of the Old Testament.
020 — WISDOM
PROVERBS
THE FEAR OF THE LORD
"The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge." Solomon's wisdom, the noble wife's portrait, and the practical instruction for living under God's covenant.
021 — WISDOM
ECCLESIASTES
VANITY OF VANITIES
"Vanity of vanities, all is vanity." The Preacher's search for meaning under the sun — pleasures, pursuits, wisdom — and the conclusion: fear God and keep his commandments.
022 — WISDOM
SONG OF SOLOMON
THE SONG OF SONGS
"Kiss me with the kisses of your mouth." A love song between Solomon and the Shulammite — an allegory of the covenant love between God and his people.
