THE RITE OF INDUCTION

Before you may access the deeper chambers of the Scriptorium, your soul must be sealed to the eternal record. The Rite of Induction binds your identity to the Archive and allows the Celestial Flame to recognize your devotion.
I.
Present Yourself — Offer your name, your region, and the title by which you wish to be known among the Scribes.
II.
Forge Your Cipher — A secret key known only to you and the Archive. Guard it well, for it is the seal of your witness.
III.
Declare Your Scholarship — The Order recognizes four degrees of knowledge. Choose the path that reflects your journey with the Word.
IV.
Affix Your Seal — Once the rite is complete, your name is written in the Lamb’s Scroll and your vigil begins.
