Garima Gospels — GAR-001
4Q119 — Septuagint Leviticus (Qumran)

SCRIBE'S GUIDE

LIGATURES

Notice how the Ge'ez letters connect compared to the block Aramaic script. Ethiopian script flows vertically with decorative ligatures, while Qumran scribes used rigid block forms.

VOWEL MARKERS

In the Ethiopian script, vowels are attached to the consonants (Abugida), whereas the ancient Dead Sea scrolls often lacked vowel points entirely, relying on context.

PUNCTUATION

Identify the "Diamond" separators in Ge'ez used to mark the end of a thought. Compare these with the paragraph markers and sentence dividers found in Qumran texts.
